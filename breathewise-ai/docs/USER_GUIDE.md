# User Guide

1. **Public Explorer:** Check live AQI, pollutant breakdown, and outdoor guidance from the home page without signing in.
2. **Account & Onboarding:** Register and complete the 5-step household setup (city, commute distance, cooking ventilation, household composition, language).
3. **Dashboard:** Switch between India NAQI and US EPA AQI, inspect hourly forecasts, and view wind/temperature inversion conditions.
4. **AI Assistant:** Ask practical daily questions (morning walks, window opening, indoor air hacks) and receive structured, source-grounded recommendations.
5. **Routine Planner & Indoor Air:** Generate an adaptive schedule, mark activities complete, and follow room-by-room checklists prioritized by cost (no-cost first).
6. **Impact & Family:** Log sustainable commutes or energy savings, verify the exact formula used, and coordinate shared household goals.
